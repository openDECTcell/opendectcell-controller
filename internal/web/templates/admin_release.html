{{icon "device-mobile"}} Release a handset identity

A handset is private to the user who registered it. If someone registered a handset that is not theirs (or that has changed hands), its real owner cannot register it until it is released. Enter the IPEI printed on the handset's label (under the battery or on the box).

You are shown whose handset it is and since when, nothing else. Releasing is recorded in the audit log with your name and the owner's. The handset is removed from every base station and from its owner's account; they register it again if it is theirs.
