Tell the controller what you are registering. Then it opens a window of one minute on every base station that is online, and you get a PIN to type into the handset.

PIN

  2. Choose the base station, then enter the PIN above.
  3. Some handsets try the PIN 0000 first and fail once; then they ask for the PIN again.
