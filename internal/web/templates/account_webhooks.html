What is sent

A call comes in, is missed or ends (with the number, the contact's name if there is one, the handset and the line); a handset is heard or not heard; the notifications of the bell. The controller does not wait for the receiver: a failed post is tried again after 1 minute, 5 minutes, 30 minutes, 2 hours and 12 hours, and then given up. A webhook that fails ten times in a row is switched off.

Every post carries X-OpenDECTcell-Signature. See wiki/Webhooks.md for the check and for the events.
